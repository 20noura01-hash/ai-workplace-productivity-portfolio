# Personal AI Integration Plan

## Workplace Scenario

Business Analyst

## 1. Meeting and Information Summarization

**Recurring Task:** Summarize meeting notes and identify key action items.

**Expected Benefit:** Reduce the time required to organize meeting information and make follow-up actions clearer.

**Preferred Prompt/Workflow:** Use a structured prompt to extract action items, owners, deadlines, priorities, and status.

**Main Risk or Verification Step:** Compare the AI output with the original meeting notes and ensure that missing information is not invented.

**Frequency:** After relevant meetings.

**What Success Looks Like:** Meeting notes are converted into a clear and accurate action-item list with less manual effort.

---

## 2. Requirements Organization

**Recurring Task:** Organize and summarize business requirements.

**Expected Benefit:** Make requirements easier to review, compare, and communicate with stakeholders.

**Preferred Prompt/Workflow:** Ask AI to organize requirements into categories and identify gaps or clarification questions.

**Main Risk or Verification Step:** Verify that the AI has not changed the meaning of the original requirements or added unsupported information.

**Frequency:** During requirements analysis.

**What Success Looks Like:** Requirements are organized clearly and potential gaps are identified earlier.

---

## 3. Professional Business Communication

**Recurring Task:** Draft stakeholder updates, clarification requests, and professional messages.

**Expected Benefit:** Improve clarity and reduce the time required to prepare routine communications.

**Preferred Prompt/Workflow:** Provide the purpose, audience, key facts, and desired tone, then review the generated draft before sending.

**Main Risk or Verification Step:** Verify all facts, dates, names, and commitments before sending the message.

**Frequency:** As needed.

**What Success Looks Like:** Communications are clear, professional, accurate, and require less time to prepare.

## Overall Integration Approach

I will use generative AI as a productivity support tool rather than a replacement for professional judgment. I will verify important information, protect confidential and personal data, and review AI-generated outputs before using them in workplace activities.
