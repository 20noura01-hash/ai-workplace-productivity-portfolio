# Responsible AI Use Checklist

## Responsible AI Principles

### 1. Accuracy

AI-generated content should be checked against reliable source information before it is used.

### 2. Accountability

The Business Analyst remains responsible for reviewing and approving the final output.

### 3. Confidentiality

Confidential organizational information should not be entered into public AI tools unless appropriate safeguards and authorization are available.

### 4. Privacy

Personal or sensitive information should be protected and should not be unnecessarily shared with AI tools.

### 5. Bias

AI-generated outputs should be reviewed for potential bias, especially when the output may affect people or business decisions.

### 6. Human Oversight

AI should support workplace tasks, while important decisions and final approval remain under human responsibility.

## Information Risk Classification

| Information Type                              | Classification | Reason                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Public company information                    | Green          | Information is publicly available and does not contain confidential data.                                |
| Internal project notes                        | Amber          | Information may contain organizational details and should be reviewed before being used with an AI tool. |
| Personal or confidential employee information | Red            | Sensitive information should not be shared with public AI tools.                                         |

## Responsible Use Statement

This portfolio does not contain real confidential, private, or sensitive organizational information. All examples are fictional or anonymized. AI-generated content was reviewed by the trainee, who takes responsibility for the final work.

## Key Learning

Generative AI can improve productivity, but it should be used with appropriate privacy, confidentiality, verification, and human oversight controls.
