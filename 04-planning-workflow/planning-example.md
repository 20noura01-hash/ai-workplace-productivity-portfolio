# Planning Workflow

## Workplace Scenario

A Business Analyst needs to organize a requirements analysis activity for a new business request.

## Goal

Complete a structured requirements analysis and prepare the findings for stakeholder review.

## Mechanisms

* Review the available business information.
* Identify stakeholders and their needs.
* Analyze and organize requirements.
* Identify gaps and clarification points.
* Prepare a structured summary.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Understand the Request

Review the business request and available information.

### Phase 2 — Analyze Requirements

Identify requirements, stakeholders, constraints, and information gaps.

### Phase 3 — Validate Information

Review unclear points and prepare clarification questions.

### Phase 4 — Prepare the Output

Create a structured requirements summary for stakeholder review.

## Tasks

### Phase 1 Tasks

* Review the initial request.
* Identify the main business objective.
* Collect available information.

### Phase 2 Tasks

* Organize the requirements.
* Identify stakeholders.
* Identify constraints and gaps.

### Phase 3 Tasks

* Review unclear requirements.
* Prepare clarification questions.
* Identify information that needs confirmation.

### Phase 4 Tasks

* Prepare the requirements summary.
* Review the document for accuracy.
* Prepare it for stakeholder discussion.

## Key Learning

Breaking a workplace goal into mechanisms, phases, and specific tasks makes the work easier to organize and track.
